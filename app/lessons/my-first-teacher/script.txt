MY FIRST TEACHER – MY DAD
Slide 1 – INTRODUCTION
00:00 Hello everyone! My name is Lê Đỗ Thanh Ngân. I am from class Flyers at SpeakWise English Centre.
00:07 Today, I want to tell you about a very special teacher in my life.
00:12 Can you guess who he is? He is my dad! His name is Lê Đặng Hiếu. He is smart, funny and creative. He is my first teacher and my best friend!
Slide 2 – WHAT DOES MY DAD TEACH ME?
00:23 My dad has taught me many things since I was little. He taught me how to walk, talk, dance and sing. As I grew older, he taught me how to stay safe, take care of myself and share my feelings.
00:37 My dad is also my special bodyguard! He takes me everywhere, from everyday school runs to exciting trips across Vietnam. I always feel safe with him.
00:48 Every year, my school has a special event called “Empathy Day”, when parents come to teach lessons in our classroom.
00:57 In Grade 1, Dad taught us how to type with ten fingers. In Grade 2, he taught us about money. This year, he taught us how to manage our time. I feel so proud when my dad teaches my class!
Slide 3 – MY FAVORITE MEMORIES WITH DAD
01:12 One of my favorite memories is learning to roller-skate with Dad. He even bought his own roller skates to practise with me. We fell down many times and got hurt, but we never gave up. We kept practising together, and we still love trying new sports!
01:31 I also love our trip to the water park in Nha Trang. We went on water slides, played with water guns and enjoyed the bubble pool. And guess what? We even got stuck in the emergency stairwell at our hotel! What a funny adventure! My dad is always ready to explore new things and discover the world with me!
Slide 4 – WHAT HAVE I LEARNED FROM HIM?
01:53 Dad teaches me to be curious, confident and creative. Most importantly, he teaches me to keep trying and never give up.  He shows me that learning is fun when we do it together.
Slide 5 – MY MESSAGE TO DAD AND ALL TEACHERS
02:06 Dear Dad, Thank you for teaching me, playing with me and always being by my side. You are my first teacher, my best friend, my special bodyguard and my biggest supporter.
02:20 I am so lucky to be your daughter! To all my teachers, thank you for your love and dedication. You inspire us to learn and grow every day.
02:30 I wish all teachers a wonderful Vietnamese Teachers’ Day! Happy November 20th!
02:37 I love you, Dad, and I love all my teachers! Thank you for listening!
